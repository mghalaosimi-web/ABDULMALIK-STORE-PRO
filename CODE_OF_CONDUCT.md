# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively, focus on technical issues, and help maintain a safe and professional collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Provide actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, customer data or personal information.
- Acknowledge other contributors' work and preserve attribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, unauthorized access, and publication of private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to the repository owner.
